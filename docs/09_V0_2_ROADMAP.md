# Mamalik v0.2 Sprint Roadmap

v0.2 continues after v0.1 is fully complete and accepted.

## Sprint 7 — Area-Type Bonuses + Map Classification

Goal:

> Real-world area type begins to affect strategy.

Main outcome:

- Kingdoms receive area-type modifiers based on stored classification.
- Area classification is visible in the kingdom dashboard.
- Bonuses are simple, explainable, and safe for balance.

## Sprint 8 — NPC / Wild Areas

Goal:

> Give protected and peaceful players meaningful non-player targets.

Main outcome:

- Players can scout and attack NPC/wild targets.
- NPC/wild targets give resources and reports, not land.
- NPC activity works without breaking player protection rules.

## Sprint 9 — Expanded Tech Tree

Goal:

> Make Knowledge more valuable and progression deeper.

Main outcome:

- Expanded tech categories for economy, agriculture, military, defense, scouting, and expansion.
- Tech effects are visible and applied in game formulas.

## Sprint 10 — Improved Scouting + Intelligence

Goal:

> Make scouting a strategic layer before war.

Main outcome:

- Scout quality tiers.
- Better report accuracy based on tech and scout count.
- Failed scout detection and defender notifications.

## Sprint 11 — Strategic Map Layers + Alliance Visuals

Goal:

> Make the map more useful for strategy.

Main outcome:

- Toggle layers for borders, alliance colors, war zones, protected kingdoms, and nearby opportunities.
- Alliance territory visualization.

## Sprint 12 — Onboarding Quests + Important Realtime Notifications

Goal:

> Improve retention and reduce confusion.

Main outcome:

- Beginner missions guide the first hours of play.
- Important event notifications can appear without requiring full realtime gameplay.

## v0.2 acceptance summary

v0.2 is accepted only when all Sprint 7–12 acceptance criteria are complete and v0.1 features still pass regression tests.
